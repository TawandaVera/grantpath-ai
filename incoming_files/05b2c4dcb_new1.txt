## ✅ Grant Search Master Prompt

**Prompt:**

> You are a professional grant research analyst. Your task is to identify **at least 100 currently open and legitimate grant opportunities** that strongly align with the criteria provided below.
>
> ### Company Criteria:
> - Industry:
> - Company size:
> - Annual revenue (if relevant):
> - Business stage (startup, growth-stage, nonprofit, research institution, etc.):
> - Geographic eligibility (country, state, region, rural/urban, etc.):
> - Target funding amount range:
> - Project focus/use of funds:
> - Minority/women/veteran-owned status (if applicable):
> - Any other eligibility factors:
>
> ---
>
> ### Strict Requirements:
>
> 1. Only include **currently open grants** (or reopening within 90 days).
> 2. Each grant must be **highly aligned** with the company criteria.
> 3. Provide the **direct link to the official grant guidelines page AND the application portal** (no generic homepage links).
> 4. Exclude:
>    - Advertisements
>    - Aggregator listing pages (unless they link directly to the official source)
>    - Expired grants
>    - Loans (unless specifically requested)
> 5. Ensure geographic eligibility is explicitly confirmed.
> 6. Prioritize federal, state, foundation, and corporate grants from legitimate institutions.
> 7. If fewer than 100 highly aligned grants are found, expand to closely related but still eligible opportunities and clearly label them as “Expanded Match.”
>
> ---
>
> ### Required Output Format (Table Format):
>
> | # | Grant Name | Funder | Funding Amount | Deadline | Geographic Eligibility | Alignment Explanation (2–3 sentences) | Official Guidelines Link | Direct Application Link |
> |---|------------|---------|----------------|----------|-----------------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
>
> ---
>
> ### Validation Step:
> After compiling the list:
> - Confirm each link leads to an official funding body.
> - Confirm the opportunity is active.
> - Remove duplicates.
> - Ensure at least 100 valid results.
>
> If necessary, conduct deep searches across:
> - Federal grant databases
> - State economic development portals
> - Private foundations
> - Corporate philanthropy sites
> - Industry-specific funding databases
>
> Provide the final curated list only (no commentary).
